# Parody Co.

Let's have some fun with the current job market. If you need a laugh today, enjoy! 🤣

Our company mission is to do something generic with legacy software that no one wants to use. We threw the term "AI" in there to appear relevant. If this excites you, read on!

Requirements:

- Strong work ethic, because we are certainly going to take advantage of you
- 10+ years experience in at least 5 programming languages
- 10+ years of experience in a framework that has only existed for 5 years
- Proven track record in the following positions
  - VP of Sales
  - Head of Marketing
  - Procurement
  - Janitorial staffing
- Fluent in at least 3 archaic spoken languages
- Knowledge of hieroglyphics, as our code base is "legacy"

Nice to haves:

- No self respect
- Willingness to work nights and weekends because you live and die for us
- Lack of morality, as we will ask you to do illegal activities

Here's what to expect in your first 30 days:

- Realizing our company is not real, yet remaining committed to working for us
- Understanding that our requirements do not meet the "entry-level" title
- Acceptance of never getting paid
- Getting to know your teammates who also enjoy a good parody of a job description

This is a fully remote position, with a strong preference for candidates who live within 2 miles of our headquarters. You know, completely not remote at all, but posting it as such.

Our commitment to you:

- Full coverage of health benefits, which we made up
- 401K that will lose you money every year
- 1 day of paid vacation per decade
- Flexible spending account that we will use to fund our parodies

The pay range for this position is $0 - $300,000. You know it's really $0.

We are an equal opportunity employer. No one has any chance, whatsoever, making it equal.

Studies have shown that certain groups will not apply unless they meet 100% of the qualifications. Even if you don't check every box, we'd encourage you to apply anyway, as we'll get a kick out of it. This is a joke after all.

Humor for the holidays: The tech job market in terms of American football. 🏈

**Interviewer**: I see that you applied to be a referee in the NFL. Why do you want this job?

**Candidate**: Because I love football and I want to ensure that it's fairly played.

**Interviewer**: How do you use AI in your rules enforcement?

**Candidate**: We use replay assist on close plays that require review.

**Interviewer**: So you haven't used ChatGPT to ask about the legality of plays or what penalties are applied?

**Candidate**: I know the rules well and can apply them on-demand. No need for AI help.

**Interviewer**: Disappointing. How fast can you run?

**Candidate**: I haven't played sports since college, but I am in good shape.

**Interviewer**: Sometimes you have to get out of the way of a play quickly. We need you to be an Olympic quality sprinter.

**Candidate**: I haven't had any problems being in the way of plays in my ten years of experience.

**Interviewer**: Disappointing. Can you bench press a linebacker?

**Candidate**: No, why is that relevant?

**Interviewer**: Sometimes there are fights, and the referees have to break them up. You may have to fight the players.

**Candidate**: So you want me to be as fast as a running back and as strong as a guard?

**Interviewer**: Yes.

**Candidate**: I have ten years of experience officiating college football games, and I know the rules inside and out. I believe the questions should revolve around that.

**Interviewer**: There are millions of people looking to be officials in the NFL. You need to stand out. It seems you aren't a good fit for the role. Goodbye.

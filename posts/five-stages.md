# Five Stages

The Five Stages of Grief, Job Search Edition

1. **Denial** - It's a numbers game. If I just keep applying, something will stick!
2. **Anger** - I know I aced that interview. Why did I get ghosted? What's wrong with them?
3. **Bargaining** - I just need to tweak my resume one more time to get past the ATS.
4. **Depression** - Is it me? Have I failed at everything I've ever done?
5. **Acceptance** - It's not me, it's the job market!

Wishing we all get to acceptance quickly. The other stages suck.
